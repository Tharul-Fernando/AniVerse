Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At AniVerse, accessible from your local server browser environment, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we record it.

Data Collection Protocols

  • Local Storage: All user interactions, including reviews and ratings, are stored locally in the browser's storage. No data is sent to external servers.
  • Network Logs: Our system does not sync inputs back to an external centralized database stack, guaranteeing full anonymous operations.
